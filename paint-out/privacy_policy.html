--- layout: politica title: Privacy Policy — Drip it Down app: Drip it Down updated: 16 November 2026 ---

Drip it Down is a puzzle game that runs on your device. In short: we collect no personal data, there are no accounts, no ads and no third-party tracking SDKs. The only thing the game sends us is anonymous usage statistics, described below — and you can turn them off.

Data we collect

No personal data. Drip it Down has no user accounts of its own and no login. We do not use advertising SDKs, and we do not use third-party analytics SDKs.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional). To tune the difficulty of the stages, Drip it Down sends us anonymous game events (for example: “a stage was started”, “a stage was won”, “a booster was used”), together with the app version, device type and the language configured on the device. This data:

Apple services the game uses

  • Game Center — if you are signed in to Game Center, your scores in the endless mode, time attack and daily challenge are sent to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple; we never receive your Apple ID. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.
  • iCloud — your progress, coins and boosters are kept in your own iCloud key-value storage so they follow you between your devices. Only your Apple ID can read it; we have no access to it.
  • In-app purchases — coin packs and the welcome pack are sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see or receive your payment details.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, stars, coins, boosters, paint, canvases and settings are stored on your device (and in your iCloud, as described above). They are never sent to us.

Children

Drip it Down collects no personal data from anyone and shows no advertising.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data and no identifier that can be linked to you, there is nothing personal for us to access, correct or delete. If you would like the anonymous statistics from your installation to stop, switch them off in Settings; deleting the app also resets the random identifier permanently.
