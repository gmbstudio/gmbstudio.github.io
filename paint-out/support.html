How do I play?

Tap an arrow and it slides out through the door of its colour. If something is in its way, it bumps back and you lose a move. Portals send pieces down to the layer below — and the order you send them in matters.

I'm stuck

Look at the layer below before you send pieces down (swipe up). In the campaign, boosters unlock as you progress: hammer, undo, brush, shuffle, bomb and wildcard.

My progress on a new device

Progress, coins and boosters are kept in your iCloud, so they follow you to any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by the App Store. To get the welcome pack back on a new device, use Restore purchases in the shop.

Something's broken

Tell us the stage number and what you were doing, and include your device model and iOS version: askme@gmbstudio.cc.
