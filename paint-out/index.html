Arrow puzzle · iPhone · iPad · Mac

Clear the board. Drip below.

Tap an arrow and it slides out through the door of its colour. What goes through a portal lands on the layer below — so every move up here decides whether the floor below can be cleared.

  • No ads, ever
  • 21 languages
  • Colour-blind friendly
  • Daily challenge

Coming soon to the App Store

One board drips into the next

Every stage is a stack of layers. Empty the top one and its paint falls to the floor below, in the order you sent it.

Rooms

Paint them back to life

Every stage you clear colours one more piece of an illustrated room: an office, a mall, an indoor pool, a lab.

Canvases

Paint by numbers

The paint you rescue from the arrows fills your pots. Use it on pixel-art canvases and win coins and boosters.

Daily

One hard stage a day

Five layers, the same for everyone. Points add up all week on Game Center; the top 3 win prizes.

For everyone

Shapes, not just colours

Every colour carries its own symbol (● ■ ★ ◆), so the game plays the same if you can’t tell colours apart.
