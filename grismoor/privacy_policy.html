Privacy Policy — Grismoor

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Grismoor is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. In short: we collect no personal data, there are no accounts, no ads and no third-party tracking SDKs. The only thing the game ever sends us is anonymous usage statistics, described below — and you can turn them off.

Data we collect

No personal data. Grismoor has no user accounts and no login. We do not use advertising SDKs, and we do not use third-party analytics SDKs.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional). To understand how far players get and which puzzles cause people to give up, Grismoor sends us anonymous game events (for example: “a scene was opened”, “a puzzle was solved”, “the Act I ending was reached”), together with the app version, device type and the language configured on the device. This data:

  • contains no identity: no name, no email, no account, no advertising identifier (IDFA) and no device identifier (IDFV). A random identifier, created locally on first launch, is used only to count devices; deleting the app resets it.
  • is received by a server operated by us that does not store your IP address or user agent, and is never shared with or sold to third parties.
  • is not used to track you across apps or websites, and is not linked to your identity.
  • can be switched off at any time in the game's Settings (“Share anonymous usage statistics”).

Launch notice (only if you ask for it)

At the end of Act I you can ask us to tell you when Acts II and III are released. Only if you tap that button does the game ask iOS for permission to send notifications and, if you allow it, store the device's notification token on our server, together with the same anonymous installation identifier described above. We use it for one thing: to send a single notification when the rest of the game is out.

  • It is not an email address, an account or a name — it is a token iOS issues for this installation, and it stops working if you delete the app.
  • We do not send marketing, and we do not share or sell it.
  • You can revoke it at any time by turning notifications off for Grismoor in iOS Settings, or by deleting the app.
  • If you never tap that button, no permission is requested and no token exists.

Data stored on your device

Your progress — which rooms you have visited, which puzzles you have solved, the items you carry, your Wit score, your language and sound preferences — is stored on your device only. It is never sent to us, and there is no cloud save.

Purchases

If you unlock the full game, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or receive your payment details. The game only records, on your device, whether the unlock is active. Restoring a purchase asks the store, not us.

Children

Grismoor is not directed at children under 13, collects no personal data from anyone, and shows no advertising.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data and no identifier that can be linked to you, there is nothing personal for us to access, correct or delete. If you would like the anonymous statistics from your installation to stop, switch them off in Settings; deleting the app also resets the random identifier permanently.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date.
