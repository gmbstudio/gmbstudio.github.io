Grismoor — Support

Stuck in the dark? Email askme@gmbstudio.cc and we'll get back to you.

I'm stuck on a puzzle

Every room hides its own clues — nothing you need is outside the scene. When you're stuck, light a candle stub: the hint points the way without giving the answer away. Stubs are hidden in every room, so look around before you ask.

How long is the free part?

Act I — the cell, the corridor, the pantry and the kitchens — is complete and free. Acts II and III are in the works; when you finish Act I you can ask the game to tell you when they land.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Grismoor runs entirely on your device and needs no connection to play.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on the device only, so deleting the app (or moving to a new phone) starts a new game.

Languages

The game is playable in Spanish and English. The narrator speaks the language you pick in Settings.

Something's broken

Tell us what room you were in and what you were doing, and include your device model and iOS/Android version: askme@gmbstudio.cc.
