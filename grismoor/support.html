Grismoor — Support

Stuck in the dark? Email askme@gmbstudio.cc and we'll get back to you.

I'm stuck on a puzzle

Every room hides its own clues — nothing you need is outside the scene. Tap the gem with the question mark in the top-right corner for a hint; ask again and the hint gets more specific, up to telling you the method. Hints are free and unlimited.

How long is the free part?

Act I — the cell, the corridor and the kitchens — is free and takes most players around an hour and a half. The story continues in Acts II and III, which are part of the full unlock.

I bought the full game and it's locked again

Open the menu → Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. The unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it never expires.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Grismoor runs entirely on your device and needs no connection to play.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on the device only, so deleting the app (or moving to a new phone) starts a new game. The full-game unlock is not lost — restore it as described above.

Languages

The game is playable in Spanish and English. The narrator speaks English; subtitles follow the language you pick in Settings.

Something's broken

Tell us what room you were in and what you were doing, and include your device model and iOS/Android version: askme@gmbstudio.cc.
