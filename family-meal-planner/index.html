Plan

The week, in minutes

Breakfast, lunch and dinner for every day. Copy last week, repeat a meal across several days, or let the assistant build the week from the style you pick for each meal.

Recipes

A library to start from

Hand-picked Mediterranean recipes with photos. Like one? One tap and it is in your own cookbook — next to grandma’s stew and everything else you add.

Import

Any recipe, in seconds

Paste a link or some text, or snap a photo or a PDF of a cookbook page, and the app fills in the fields. Cook Mode then shows the steps full-screen and keeps the screen awake.

Shopping

The list builds itself

Every ingredient of the week, duplicates merged, split across your stores — bakery, market, supermarket — and the app remembers where you buy each thing.

Family

One menu, every phone

Invite your family with a code. The menu and the list sync instantly: when someone ticks off the bread, everyone knows.

Offline

No signal at the store?

Everything lives on your device, so the list is there in the aisle. Dark mode, and English, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Portuguese.

Free and ad-free

The whole app is free: planning, recipes, import, shopping lists and family sharing. The free version shows ads. If you’d rather not see them, a one-time purchase in Settings removes them for good — no subscription.

Recipe import is unlimited when your device can read the recipe on its own. When it needs our server, you get five imports a day, and a short video adds three more.

Your recipes are yours

There is no account to create: your recipes, menus and lists stay on your device. They only travel to our servers (in the EU) if you choose to share with your family, and only your family sees them. Importing a recipe works on the device where it can; otherwise only the item you import is sent to be read, never your library.

The free version’s ads come from Google AdMob, and in Europe they are personalised only if you say yes. The app also sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can fix things — never your recipes — and you can switch the statistics off in Settings. The details are in the privacy policy.
