Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Family Meal Planner ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used. In short: your recipes and plans stay on your device, we don't ask for an account, and the ads we show do not track you.

1. Data you create

Recipes, meal plans and shopping lists you add are stored locally on your device (SQLite). This content is not uploaded to our servers and is not shared with anyone.

2. No account, no personal profile

You don't sign up. To make the app work, an anonymous identifier may be created (Supabase) — it is not linked to your name, email or any personal identity, and we do not collect contact details.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob. We request non-personalized (contextual) ads only: no cross-app tracking, and no advertising identifier (IDFA) is used, so the app does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. AdMob may process limited data (such as coarse, non-identifying signals and IP address) to serve and measure contextual ads, per Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics & diagnostics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate, non-identifying usage (which features are used) and to receive crash/diagnostic reports, so we can improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity.

5. In-app purchase

You can buy a one-time "Remove ads" purchase through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Apple / Google — we never see your payment details.

6. Data we do not collect

No name, no email, no contacts, no precise location, and nothing that identifies you personally. We do not sell data.

7. Your choices

  • Delete everything: uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data, or delete items inside the app.
  • Remove ads: buy the one-time "Remove ads" purchase in Settings.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions? Email askme@gmbstudio.cc.
