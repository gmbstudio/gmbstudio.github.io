Delete your account & data

Family Meal Planner — by gmbStudio. Last updated: July 27, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Family Meal Planner account and the data associated with it. Using an account in Family Meal Planner is optional — the app works fully as a guest.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to askme@gmbstudio.cc from the email address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Family Meal Planner account".
  3. We verify the request and delete your account and associated profile data. This is completed within 30 days, and you receive a confirmation.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the profile data stored in our backend (Supabase):

  • Your email address (account identifier)
  • Your display name
  • Your profile photo (avatar)
  • Your anonymous user identifier

What stays on your device

Your recipes, meal plans and shopping lists are stored locally on your device, not in our backend. To remove them, delete the items inside the app or uninstall the app — uninstalling removes all locally stored data.

Data retention

We do not retain account/profile data after deletion, except where a limited retention period is required by law (for example, records related to fraud prevention or legal obligations). No additional retention periods apply to the profile data listed above.

Questions

Contact us at askme@gmbstudio.cc.
