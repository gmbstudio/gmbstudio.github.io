Getting started

  1. Open Recipes and tap + to add your recipes — name, ingredients and steps.
  2. Go to Plan, pick a day and assign a recipe to breakfast, lunch or dinner.
  3. Open Shopping to get a list generated automatically from your week's meals — check items off as you shop.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. The app works right away and fully offline — your recipes, meal plans and shopping lists are stored on your device.

Where is my data stored?
Locally on your device. We don't upload your recipes or meal plans to our servers. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I remove the ads?
Open Settings → Remove ads and complete the one-time purchase. Already bought it? Use Restore purchases on the same screen.

Do the ads track me?
No. We serve non-personalized (contextual) ads only — no cross-app tracking and no advertising identifier.

How do I delete my data?
Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. You can also clear individual recipes, plans and lists inside the app.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone (iOS) and Android.
